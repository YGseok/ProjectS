HOW THIS PACK WAS MADE  (AI disclosure)

  This pack contains AI-assisted artwork. AI image tools were used as a starting
  point and as visual reference, and the sprites were then corrected by hand -
  which is where most of the work went.

  Every frame was checked and reworked individually. Frames were rejected and
  redone until the animation was right. The corrections included fixing which leg
  was which across the cycle, fixing the depth order so the near leg is drawn in
  front of the far one, repairing the hip joints where the legs did not connect
  to the body, correcting the light and shadow so the near leg stays lighter
  throughout, restoring the thigh-high stockings where they had been drawn as
  tights, and rebuilding the walk mechanics so the body drops as the legs spread
  and the feet stay on a consistent ground line.

  The character design, art direction, animation timing and frame-by-frame
  corrections are the author's own.
